The people who review and merge changes to `apb` and cut its releases.

| Sol Aitken | [@solaitken](https://github.com/solaitken) | Lead maintainer: day-to-day development, reviews, pull requests |
| Serg Eroshe | [@itechmeat](https://github.com/itechmeat) | Maintainer and project owner: direction, releases, repository settings |

## How to reach us

- A bug or an idea: [open an issue](https://github.com/itechmeat/agentic-playbooks/issues).
- A change: open a pull request and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Both maintainers are asked for review through [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).
- A vulnerability: report it privately as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says, never in a public issue.
